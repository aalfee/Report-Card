# Brand System Overview

This document describes ALFIYAY's brand identity, visual style, and voice.

## Brand positioning

ALFIYAY is an accessible document platform for mission-oriented teams. Its brand personality is confident, clear, and warm.

## Visual direction

- Use simple, modern layouts.
- Prioritize readability and calm color palettes.
- Keep icons and text consistent across interfaces.

## Voice

- Use plain language.
- Be direct and respectful.
- Make guidance easy to follow.
