# ALFIYAY Master Vision

ALFIYAY is a digital document hub designed to help institutional partners store, share, and manage their most important assets in a secure, searchable, and user-friendly way.

## Vision

Create a modern, accessible repository for vision documents, policies, and brand materials that can be published publicly and managed privately.

## Mission

- Make verified documents easy to discover for approved audiences.
- Keep public and private content separate.
- Use a single source of truth for document metadata.

## Values

- Predictability: documents live in stable folders and follow naming rules.
- Transparency: metadata and access levels are explicit.
- Security: private content is protected and public content is easy to share.
